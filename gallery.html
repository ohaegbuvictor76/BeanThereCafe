From Bean to Cup

A four-step exhibit of how your coffee is made.

Step 1

The Bean

Every cup starts as a small seed from a farm. We choose beans with a sweet, nutty flavour.

Step 2

The Roast

Heat turns the seeds brown and brings out their flavour. We roast in small batches every week.

Step 3

The Brew

Hot water is pushed through finely ground coffee. The result is a rich, golden espresso.

Step 4

The Pour

Our barista steams the milk and pours it in. Your drink arrives fresh and hot.
